Arcade privacy policy

Prepared October 1, 2026. Arcade is provided by Davidfromkansas. Contact david.lietjauw@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information Arcade stores

Arcade stores the authenticated user identifier supplied by its hosting platform, your chosen display name and username, your background preference (including an image if you upload one), conversation identifiers and titles, conversation membership, message text, sender identifiers, timestamps, and read positions used for unread indicators. Support emails contain the information you choose to send.

Why information is used

This information supports sign-in and account association, directory search, message delivery and history, access checks, unread indicators, personalization, troubleshooting, and support.

Who can see information

Other signed-in Arcade users can find your name and username in the directory and start conversations with you. Conversation members can see messages, sender names and usernames, and participant lists. Arcade checks conversation membership before returning message history. Your background setting is personal and is not shared with conversation participants.

Arcade runs on the ChatGPT Sites hosting platform and uses its database. The operator and hosting providers may process stored information to operate and support the service. Using Arcade through ChatGPT or Codex sends tool results, which may contain profile information, conversation previews, and message content, through those services. Their own policies also apply. Arcade does not implement end-to-end encryption. Recipients may copy or share messages outside Arcade.

Retention and deletion

The current application has no automatic expiry for profiles or messages and no self-service deletion endpoint. Stored records persist unless manually removed. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the address above. The operator must assess and handle requests manually. We do not specify a hosting-provider backup or log retention period here because it has not been verified. Removing an account or disconnecting the plugin does not by itself erase stored Arcade data or copies held by recipients.

Other processing

The current Arcade application code contains no advertising, payment processing, third-party analytics, or model-training feature. Hosting and connected services may maintain operational or error logs under their own policies. This statement does not describe or override those providers’ independent practices.

Your choices

Choose a display name and username you are comfortable sharing with other Arcade users. Avoid sending sensitive information. You can change your background in the app, stop using Arcade, disconnect its integration through the host application, or contact us about your data. Disconnecting the integration is separate from a deletion request.
