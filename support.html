Arcade support

Arcade is provided by Davidfromkansas. For help, bug reports, privacy questions, or account/data deletion requests, email david.lietjauw@gmail.com.

Describe what happened, the device or app you used, and your Arcade username if relevant. Screenshots can help, but remove private messages and personal information that are not needed. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or access tokens.

Privacy and deletion requests

State which account and data your request concerns. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account before acting on it. Arcade currently has no self-service account or message deletion controls. Requests require manual assessment; this page does not promise a specific response time or automatic erasure of recipients’ copies.

Problems with another user

Report unwanted contact or abusive content by email, providing only the context needed to investigate. Arcade currently has no in-app blocking or reporting controls.
