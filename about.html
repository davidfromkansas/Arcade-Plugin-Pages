About Arcade

Arcade is a messaging app by Davidfromkansas. Create a name and username, find other Arcade users, and start direct or group conversations. Open your messages from the sidebar or alongside a conversation, and choose a personal chat background.

Arcade is free and has no purchases. Sign in with ChatGPT to use messaging. Your name and username are discoverable by other signed-in Arcade users, who can start conversations with you.

For help, contact david.lietjauw@gmail.com.
